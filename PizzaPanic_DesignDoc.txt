Title: Pizza Panic - Game Design Overview

High Concept:
You are an overworked pizza shop employee in Monsterville, cooking and delivering pizzas to monster-infested neighborhoods.

Core Gameplay Loop:
1. Receive an order
2. Prepare and cook the pizza
3. Deliver the pizza through Monsterville while avoiding or fending off monsters

Core Mechanics:
- Real-time pizza cooking minigame (topping placement + bake timing)
- Vehicle or on-foot delivery through an open map
- Monsters are attracted to the player and can attack them, the vehicle, or the pizza
- Pizza quality degrades if it is damaged during delivery
- Deliveries are timed—late deliveries result in reduced payout

Victory Condition:
Deliver pizza to every neighborhood in Monsterville and win their hearts. Each zone may require a unique pizza to pacify its local monsters.

Failure:
The player can die, triggering a setback such as loss of money or reputation. This is not a hard game over—the player can continue after penalties.

Music:
- Overall Feel: Lofi, nostalgia-based.
- Influences: Vaporwave, old Weather Channel music, 80s synth.
- Specific Track Vibes: Phil Collins "In the Air Tonight", late-night drive atmosphere.

Future Expansion Concepts:
- Neighborhood progression and map unlocking
- More complex cooking mechanics and custom pizza recipes
- Monster variety, behaviors, and factions
- Vehicle and shop upgrades
- Defensive systems (fortify the pizza joint)
- Randomized order events and modifiers

Ideas:
- Pizza as a weapon:
    - Melee attack (whacking)
    - Ranged boomerang throw (possibly magical)
    - Pizza shield (defensive)
- Topping effects:
    - Different toppings grant the pizza special properties when used as a weapon/shield (e.g., peppers burn monsters, mushrooms increase durability/defense).
        - Onions: Stun/disorient nearby monsters (like tear gas).
        - Extra Cheese: Increases melee knockback/shield durability, but slows throws.
        - Garlic: Effective against specific monsters (e.g., vampires).
- Topping Combinations: Combining specific toppings could unlock unique effects (e.g., Pepperoni + Peppers = Flaming Grease attack).
- Weapon/Tool Degradation: Using the pizza offensively/defensively degrades its quality faster, creating a risk/reward trade-off.
- Pizza Box: Placing the pizza in a box could offer additional protection (reducing degradation/damage) but might prevent using its special properties/attacks.
- Horror Twist during Travel: Incorporate horror elements during delivery segments, such as encountering large, imposing monsters partially obscured by fog or darkness, creating a sense of dread and vulnerability. (Needs more thought).
