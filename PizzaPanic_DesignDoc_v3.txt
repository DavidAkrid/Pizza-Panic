CONCEPT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A first-person co-op "friendslop" game. You and your friends run a pizza shop
in a monster-infested neighborhood. You cook. You deliver. You survive.

The game is the platform. The experience with your friends is the point.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PILLARS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. FRIENDS ARE THE FUN
   Systems exist to create stories, chaos, and funny moments between players.
   If a feature doesn't make friends yell, laugh, or help each other, question it.

2. SILLY BUT SCARY
   Real threat, never fully serious. Tone references:
   Lethal Company, REPO, Content Warning.

3. YOU HAVE A JOB TO DO
   You're not outside to explore or fight. You're delivering a pizza.
   The job is what pushes you out the door and into trouble.

4. THE NEIGHBORHOOD GROWS WITH YOU
   Outside starts as an unfamiliar, dangerous place where you're defenseless.
   Over time you learn it, gear up, and it becomes your turf.
   Navigation reference: Schedule I.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FORMAT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- First-person 3D
- Online co-op (target: 1–4 players)
- Engine: Godot 4 (GDScript)
- Look: low-poly models, low-res / PSX-style rendering, heavy use of darkness,
  fog, and sound. Cheap to produce, hides rough art, fits the tone.
- Platform: PC (Steam), with an itch.io demo first

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WORLD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
One continuous neighborhood map. No loading screens between shop and street.

The shop is the anchor: warm, lit, safe. Monsters can't come in.
Outside is not one fixed mood. What it feels like depends on:
  - Where you're going (close and familiar vs. far and unknown)
  - What the job is (simple drop-off vs. something weird)
  - What you have (on foot and empty-handed vs. geared up in a vehicle)
  - Who's with you (alone vs. with friends)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CORE LOOP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Orders come in at the shop
2. Make the pizza
3. Go out and deliver it
4. Get back to the shop, get paid
5. Spend money on upgrades, gear, vehicles
6. Repeat, going further and handling harder jobs

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
THE SHOP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Safe space. Cozy, warm, a relief to come back to.
- Cooking is simple and physical (grab, place, bake). It should be quick,
  and a little messy with friends, not a deep cooking sim.
- Pizza quality affects payout.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OUTSIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Navigate the neighborhood to the delivery address.
- Monsters are out there and are drawn to you and your pizza.
- Monsters can damage the pizza, knock you around, or take you out.
- Early game: you're defenseless. Avoid, hide, run.
- Late game: you have tools to deal with threats, and going out becomes
  more fun than scary. Danger never fully goes away. Further and stranger
  jobs keep raising the stakes.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PROGRESSION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Earn money from deliveries. Spend it, find things, get stronger.

Categories:
- Weapons      (start improvised and silly: rolling pin, pizza cutter)
- Vehicles     (on foot → skateboard → bike → delivery van, etc.)
- Gadgets      (flashlights, tools that help you navigate or escape)
- Upgrades     (to yourself and to the shop)

Some things are bought; some are found out in the neighborhood.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CO-OP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Co-op is the target experience. Ideas to explore (not committed):
- Players naturally split roles: someone cooks, someone delivers
- Rescuing a friend who's stuck or downed outside
- Carrying multiple pizzas together on a big order
- Proximity voice chat (a core ingredient of the reference games)

DEVELOPMENT PLAN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PHASE 1 — Single-player proof of concept (timebox: 2–3 weeks)
  Purpose: find out if the loop and the atmosphere are fun.
  This build is throwaway. Lessons, art, audio, and layouts carry over;
  code mostly does not.

  Milestone 1 scope:
  - First-person movement
  - Shop interior with warm lighting
  - Simple pizza making
  - Walk out the door into a dark neighborhood (no load)
  - One delivery address
  - One monster type that hunts you and damages the pizza
  - Walk back, get paid (money is just a number on screen)

  NOT in Phase 1: progression, weapons, vehicles, menus, saving.

  Success = it's fun for 5 minutes and stepping outside feels different
  from being in the shop.

PHASE 2 — Multiplayer spike
  Two players walking around the same shop together, online.
  Prove the networking before building anything else on top.

PHASE 3 — Rebuild on a multiplayer foundation
  Rebuild Phase 1's loop as co-op, then start adding progression.
