2. HOW CO-OP WORKS (the one idea to understand)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Every player's computer runs a full copy of the world. To keep the copies in agreement:

  THE HOST OWNS THE WORLD.
    Items, stations, the oven/fryer, the order, team money, upgrades, monsters.
    Only the host changes these. Godot's "MultiplayerSynchronizer" nodes copy the
    results to everyone automatically.

  EACH PLAYER OWNS THEIR OWN BODY.
    Your movement, where you look, your flashlight, which slot you have selected.
    Your machine sends those to everyone else.

  TO CHANGE THE WORLD, YOU ASK THE HOST.
    Pressing E on a station, picking up an item, knocking, buying an upgrade: your machine
    sends a request (an "RPC") to the host. The host checks it's allowed (close enough?
    room in your inventory? did someone else grab it first?), applies it, and the result
    syncs back out. That's why two people can never grab the same pizza.

  "SPAWNERS" create things on every machine at once (players, items, monsters), with the
    same name and starting data everywhere, so requests always find the right object.

How players connect lives in ONE file: scripts/net.gd. Today it's direct connections
(host's IP address, port 7777). Swapping to Steam lobbies later only changes that file.

3. WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU START
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Autoloads start: InputSetup registers all controls; Net gets ready to connect.
2. world.tscn loads on every machine: the shop, the neighborhood (built by code), the HUD,
   the music, and the menu (Host game / address box / Join game / Quit).
3. HOST GAME: Net opens port 7777. The host's player spawns, GameState creates the first
   order (one pizza to 12 Main St), 9 monsters spawn, and the screen shows the addresses
   friends can type (home network, plus Tailscale if it's running).
4. JOIN GAME: Net connects to the typed address. When the host sees the new connection it
   spawns that player, and the joiner receives everything that already exists.
5. If the host leaves, everyone else goes back to the menu. Esc opens the pause menu
   (the world keeps running) with Leave game / Quit.

4. THE GAME LOOP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A. ORDER (game_state.gd)
   One shared order at a time: 1-2 items from pizza (1-2 toppings), mozzarella sticks, soda.
   Shown on the kitchen board, everyone's HUD and compass, and as a lit porch + gold chevron.
   A new order arrives whenever someone is in the shop and there's no current order.

B. COOK (station.gd, cook_station.gd)
   Dough -> topping bins -> oven (dings at PERFECT, 8-11s) -> boxes.
   Freezer -> fryer (dings at GOLDEN, 5-7s). Cooler -> soda. Trash.
   Everyone sees the same oven/fryer countdown and hears the hum, sizzle and ding.

C. INVENTORY (item.gd, player.gd)
   2 slots (up to 6 with the Bigger Bag upgrade). Scroll or 1-6 to switch.
   Every pizza/can/basket is a real item that remembers who holds it and in which slot,
   so everyone's inventory looks the same on every machine. Friends see what's in your hand.

D. OUTSIDE
   Leaving the shop makes YOUR screen foggy and dark and switches YOUR music to the drone.
   Unboxed pizza you carry loses 1% every 3 seconds outside.

E. MONSTERS (shambler.gd)
   Run by the host. Each chases the closest player who smells of food (14m) or is close
   (6m), never inside the shop. Bites knock you back and take 34% off your pizza.
   Only the hunted player hears the heartbeat.

F. FIGHTING (combat.gd)
   Left click punch/swing, right click throw, hold to charge, other button cancels.
   Monsters get the full force. Friends only get a SHOVE: no damage to them or their stuff.
   Swinging a pizza still hurts YOUR pizza (15%); thrown pizzas take 3-15% on landing.

G. DELIVERY (house_door.gd, game_state.gd)
   Knock at the order house: everyone sees the door crack open and the customer lean out
   (looking at you on your screen). They take matching items from EVERYONE within 4.5m of
   the door, so you can split an order between players. Pay goes to the team.
   Pizza: $20 x toppings x bake x condition (+$10 tip if perfect). Sticks: $8 x fry. Soda: $3.

H. UPGRADES (upgrade_station.gd)
   Right wall of the shop. Personal upgrades, paid from team money; plaques show YOUR prices.

5. THE SCRIPTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
net.gd             The ONLY connection code (host/join/leave). Swap for Steam here.
world.gd           The world scene: menu, pause menu, spawning players/items/monsters,
                   your inside/outside mood, HUD updates, cold-pizza rule (host).
game_state.gd      Host-owned rules: order, team money, deliveries, upgrades. Synced.
player.gd          Your body: movement, stamina, camera, flashlight, inventory, shoves,
                   footsteps you hear from friends, upgrade effects.
combat.gd          Charged punch/swing/throw and who gets hit.
item.gd            Every holdable thing: cans, pizzas, sticks. Holder, slot, state, physics.
pizza.gd           What a pizza is (toppings, stage, bake, condition) and its 3D look.
station_base.gd    "Ask the host to use this station" plumbing shared by all stations.
station.gd         Dough, toppings, boxes, trash, cooler, freezer.
cook_station.gd    The oven and the fryer.
upgrade_station.gd The upgrade plaques.
house.gd           Porch light + chevron.
house_door.gd      Knocking and the customer visit.
shambler.gd        Monster brain (host) and animation (everyone).
neighborhood.gd    Builds the whole town in code (same on every machine).
hud.gd, compass.gd The on-screen overlay.
ambience.gd        Placeholder music, generated live.
dev_test.gd        Automated two-window tests (see section 7).

6. WHERE TO TWEAK THINGS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Movement, stamina ............... player.gd: WALK_SPEED, SPRINT_SPEED, STAMINA_*
Punch/swing/throw, charge ....... combat.gd: PUNCH, CHARGE_*, THROW_SPEED, PLAYER_SHOVE_MULT
Item weapon & throw stats ....... item.gd: weapon(), throw_stats()
Pizza damage (swing, thrown) .... item.gd: PIZZA_SWING_DAMAGE, PIZZA_THROW_DAMAGE
Cold pizza ...................... world.gd: COLD_DAMAGE, COLD_INTERVAL
Monsters ........................ shambler.gd: CHASE_SPEED, SMELL_RANGE, BITE_DAMAGE, ...
Pay, tips, order mix ............ game_state.gd: PAY, PERFECT_TIP, ITEM_WEIGHTS, MAX_ORDER_ITEMS
Who hands over at the door ...... game_state.gd: DELIVERY_RANGE
Upgrade names/prices ............ game_state.gd: UPGRADES (effects: player.gd apply_upgrades)
Oven / fryer timing ............. cook_station.gd: TIMES
Customer animation .............. house_door.gd: DOOR_OPEN, PEEK_LEAN, _play_visit
Map, houses, lamps, signs ....... neighborhood.gd
Keybindings ..................... input_setup.gd (also update the Controls board!)
Shop layout ..................... world.tscn (move things in the Godot editor)
Port .............................. net.gd: DEFAULT_PORT (or run with  -- --port=<n>)

7. PLAYING, TESTING, BUILDING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Play in the editor: open game_coop/project.godot, press F5, Host game.
  For a second player, run a second copy (or an exported build) and Join with a blank address.
- Builds go to build/coop/: PizzaPanicCoop.exe (Windows), PizzaPanicCoop.x86_64 (Linux,
  chmod +x it), PizzaPanicCoop_mac.zip (Mac, unsigned: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Open Anyway, or  xattr -cr "/path/to/app").
- Joining from another house: both install Tailscale; join with the host's 100.x address
  (shown on screen when hosting). Same Wi-Fi: use the 10.x / 192.168.x address.
  First time hosting, Windows Firewall asks: click Allow.
- Automated tests (used by Claude): one copy runs  -- --test=host --port=7778,  another
  -- --test=client --port=7778. Temporary checks go into dev_test.gd with
  tools/devtest_checks.py and are removed after.

8. KNOWN LIMITS / NOT THERE YET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Joining needs an IP address (Steam lobbies/invites later - no store page needed to start,
  Valve's test app ID 480 works).
- No saving: money and upgrades reset each session.
- No voice chat yet (proximity voice is a big part of the genre - later).
- Monsters steer straight at you (no pathfinding).
- Placeholder art (boxes and capsules) and placeholder audio.
- Orders max out at 2 items even with more slots.
- Not yet tested over a real internet connection with a friend (only same PC / same network).
