THE PITCH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A first-person co-op "friendslop" game for 1-4 players.

You roll into Monsterville as a nobody and get handed the keys to a run-down pizza shop at
the edge of a town where the sun never rises. Cook. Deliver. Survive. Help the residents,
push back the dark one street at a time, and earn your way across town.

The end goal of the whole game: BRING BACK THE MORNING.

The game is the platform. The experience with your friends is the point.

PILLARS

1. FRIENDS ARE THE FUN
   Systems exist to create stories, chaos and funny moments between players.
   If a feature doesn't make friends yell, laugh, argue or help each other, question it.

2. SILLY BUT SCARY
   Real threat, never fully serious. Refs: Lethal Company, REPO, Content Warning.

3. YOU HAVE A JOB TO DO
   You're out there to deliver pizza. The job is what pushes you out the door.

4. SAFE INSIDE, NIGHTMARE OUTSIDE
   The shop is warm, lit and safe. Outside is dark. The core feeling is:
   safe shop -> dread outside -> relief back inside.

5. A NOBODY BECOMES THE TOWN'S HERO
   Progress is permanent and visible. Streets you've lit stay lit, neighbors you've helped
   remember you, and the town physically changes as you save it. Ref: Schedule I.

STORY & SETTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Monsterville: a town stuck in endless night. The morning hasn't come in years.
- The pizza shop is the one place still open, and its crew the only people out after dark.
- Residents (human and otherwise) are hiding in their homes. They still order pizza.
- Each neighborhood has named residents with problems. Helping them earns trust, tools,
  and access to the rest of town.
- Saving neighborhoods weakens the night. The final story chain brings back the sunrise.
- The shop radio is the narrator: night announcements, jokes, story hints
  ("Gooood evening, Monsterville! It's night 5, and the moon's still stuck...").

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
THE NIGHT CYCLE (one shift)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
It is always night. There is no daytime to "play it safe" in.

1. OPEN
   The neon sign buzzes to OPEN, the radio announces the night, orders start coming in.

2. NIGHT (normal play, ~15 min)
   Take orders, cook, deliver, do odd jobs. The night gets gradually harder as it goes:
   more monsters awake, more aggressive.

3. LAST CALL (~2 min warning)
   Clock tower bell, the sky turns red, the radio warns everyone. Get back to the shop.
   The sign flips to CLOSED automatically; the door locks behind the last one in.

4. DEEP NIGHT
   OUTSIDE: a nightmare. A nastier, random monster pool comes out (Lethal-style: different
   each night). The kidnapper hunts. Deep night SPECIAL ORDERS appear: huge pay, huge risk.
   "Is it worth it? Is it safe? Can we make it?"
   INSIDE: the break. Count money, restock, buy, prep for tomorrow, read mail, check the
   town map, play the arcade machine. Sometimes something knocks on the door. Don't open it.

5. CLOCK IN -> NEXT NIGHT
   Every player punches their time card at the punch clock ("2/3 clocked in").
   Only works with everyone inside. When the last person clocks in, time skips in place
   (no fade to black): the wall clock spins, the red sky drains, the horror sounds fade,
   the radio crackles on, the sign buzzes to OPEN. ~5-8 seconds.

No quota. No game over. A bad night means less money and progress, never a reset.

ORDERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ORDER TERMINAL (front counter)
- A chunky green CRT screen. Orders arrive on their own with a beep, at a pace set by
  reputation and time of night. Several wait at once.
- Each shows: address + neighborhood, rough distance, items, pay, an expiry countdown,
  and sometimes a NOTE in the customer's voice ("DO NOT KNOCK. BABY IS SLEEPING.
  LEAVE ON PORCH." / "extra olives or else"). Notes carry personality and special requests.
- Deep night special orders flash red.
- Walk up, press E to accept. Anyone can accept. No limit: the real limit is what the
  team can physically cook and carry. Overcommitting is your own fault.

EXPIRY & PENALTIES
- Ignored orders just expire. No real penalty (maybe a tiny dip for ignoring lots).
- Accepted but not delivered in time: reputation penalty in that neighborhood.

KITCHEN SCREEN (where the chalkboard is now)
- Big and clear. Accepted orders appear automatically with their countdowns.
- The cook checks off a whole order as "ready to go". Delivered orders clear off.
- The terminal is where you DECIDE; the kitchen screen is where you WORK.

DELIVERY & PAY
- Knock; the customer peeks out and takes the order from everyone near the door
  (team deliveries, partial deliveries allowed). (Exists in game_coop.)
- Base pay x accuracy x cooking quality x condition.
- Tips: random, but faster + better deliveries push the odds toward bigger tips.
- HUD + compass show accepted orders.

THE SHOP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
COOKING (exists): dough, toppings, oven, boxes, freezer, fryer, soda cooler, trash.

RESTOCKING
- Ingredients run out (bins visibly empty). Restock costs a little money.
- Gives the deep night break a purpose: get the shop ready for tomorrow.

PREP
- Pre-make dough, set up the kitchen during the break -> a faster next night.

THE SUPPLY CATALOG (one supplier for everything, by the back door)
- Order during the break; it's at the back door when the next night opens.
  (Who delivers it in Monsterville? A running radio joke.)
- KITCHEN: ingredients, boxes, frozen foods, soda, new recipes/toppings.
- REPAIR & UTILITY: lightbulbs, fuses, duct tape, flashlight batteries, flares,
  bait slices, air horn, traps (roach motel, bear trap), AD POSTERS.
- GEAR: insulated delivery bag (slower cooling), weapons (rolling pin, pizza peel,
  frying pan), protection (padded jacket, hard hat), first aid kits.
- TRAVEL: skateboard, bike, van, gas.
- SHOP: new/better stations, decorations, arcade games, jukebox songs.
- COSMETICS: hats, aprons, uniforms, shop decor.

THE SHOP GROWS
- Second oven, bigger fridge, faster stations, better ingredients: better food, faster.
- LATE GAME: buy a second shop deeper in town (a forward base).
- LATE GAME: the FOOD TRUCK, a kitchen on wheels. One cooks, one drives.

EMPLOYEE AREA
- Punch clock (ready check).
- The LOCKER: every key tool you've unlocked is always available here (die with the
  wrench, grab another). Cosmetics/outfits live here too.
- Bathroom: where you respawn (see Players).

PLAYERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HEALTH & DEATH
- Players have health. Monster bites hurt you AND your pizza.
- At zero you go DOWN: you can crawl and call for help.
- A teammate can REVIVE you within ~30s (upgrades make it faster / easier).
- Not revived (or solo): you respawn at the shop by stepping out of a BATHROOM STALL
  (toilet flush). You lose what you were carrying and pay a hospital bill.

THE KIDNAPPER (deep night monster)
- Grabs a downed or unlucky player and drags them to its lair.
- The captive can watch, bang on the walls, and yell.
- The team has limited time to rescue them. Fail, and the toilet flushes.
- Forces the choice: finish the delivery, or go get your friend?

COMBAT (exists)
- Charged punch / swing / throw. Monsters take full force.
- Friendly fire is a shove only: no damage to friends or their stuff.
- Combat is never the point. Upgrades make you less helpless, not powerful.

FOOD BUFFS (your product is the power-up)
- Soda: stamina boost.
- Each topping gives a buff, e.g. Pepperoni = stronger punches, Peppers (spicy) = faster,
  Mushroom = see better in the dark, Olive = take less damage,
  Mozzarella sticks = less stamina drain.
- Mixed pizzas stack buffs. Better cooking = longer buffs.
- Eat anywhere, including mid-chase (takes a couple of seconds).
- Every pizza you eat is one you didn't deliver. A natural "cook makes buff pizzas for
  the runners" role.

THE TOWN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ONE BIG MAP
- Starts with the block around the shop (what exists now). More neighborhoods open up
  through story tasks and key tools (broken bridge, locked gate, collapsed tunnel).
- Distance matters: later neighborhoods need a skateboard, bike or van to reach in time.

NEIGHBORHOODS (first-pass pitches; each = monster pool + delivery twist + residents)
- The Starter Block: shamblers. Learn the basics.
- Graveyard Suburbs: ghosts that pass through walls but freeze when looked at.
  One player watches while another delivers.
- The High-Rise: deliveries go UP. Stairwell monsters; a slow, loud elevator.
- The Swamp: fog so thick the compass glitches. Stilt shacks, boats, plank bridges.
- The Rich Part of Town: gated mansions, guard-dog monsters, gate codes on order notes,
  big tips.
- The Carnival: the customer is inside a funhouse; clown monsters copy your movement.
- Downtown: traffic as hazard and tool, rival delivery drivers.

MONSTERS
- Each neighborhood has its own monster pool.
- Deep night adds to or replaces it with scarier monsters, random each night.
- Lamp smashers (deep night) break streetlights. Later: light-loving monsters (giant moths).

DELIVERY TWISTS (anywhere)
- "Leave it on the porch", timed orders, giant two-person party orders, customers who are
  monsters, orders that make monsters angrier while you carry them.

SAVING A NEIGHBORHOOD (several ways it improves)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LIGHT (hands-on)
- Buy bulbs, fix broken streetlights (needs a tool like a ladder).
- Lit areas: monsters less likely to be there, not impossible. Deep night monsters care less.
- Portable lanterns: placeable anywhere, pricier, can be knocked over.
- Upkeep: bulbs flicker then burn out; lamp smashers break them.
- The group builds its own lit safe routes. Ref: placing torches in Minecraft.

REPUTATION (per neighborhood, stars)
- Raised by good deliveries, helping residents, fixing lights, odd jobs.
- Good rep: more orders, bigger tips, residents' story tasks open up.
- Bad rep: fewer orders, smaller tips, rude notes on orders, story tasks pause.
  Never a lockout: there's a floor, and you can always earn it back.

STORY TASKS (milestones)
- Residents' task chains. Example: the bridge is broken; the plumber has the tools but
  his basement is flooded with monsters. Deliver his pizza, help him out, he gives you
  the WRENCH (opens the bridge, better weapon, can fix things around town).
- A neighborhood's final task (e.g. fixing its POWER BOX) lights it permanently:
  saved for good, even lamp smashers can't undo it.

AD POSTERS
- Buy a pack (e.g. $50 for 5), put them up on poles/fences/walls.
- That area places more orders for a few nights. Wear out over time / torn by monsters.
- Work better under a working streetlight.
- Later: coupon posters (faster rep), weird posters for deep night customers.

ODD JOBS (quick money + rep, filler between orders)
- Trash bags to the dumpster, roaches/pests, fixing streetlights, lost pets, lost mail,
  small favors.

CO-OP & SAVING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- The game is the HOST'S TOWN. It saves after every night.
- The host's save also keeps each friend's upgrades, cosmetics and held items.
- Players need a stable identity to get their stuff back: a name box on the menu for now,
  Steam accounts later.
- Progress is permanent. Setbacks are local (lost cargo, a bill, lost rep), never a reset.
- 4 players max (host + 3).
