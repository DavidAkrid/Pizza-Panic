Title: Pizza Panic
Version: 2 (Prototype Design)
Date: 2026-04-08

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CONCEPT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
You are the sole proprietor of a pizza shop in a monster-infested neighborhood.
You cook. You deliver. You survive.

The game is built on two emotional states in constant alternation:

  SAFE (kitchen) → DREAD (delivery) → RELIEF (return) → repeat

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WORLD / MAP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
One continuous open map. No loading screens. No scene cuts.

The shop sits on the map as your anchor. Inside: warm light, cozy music, safety.
Outside: dark streets, ambient dread, monsters lurking.

The tonal shift is spatial — you feel it the moment you step out the front door.
Addresses (delivery targets) are scattered across the neighborhood. The further
the address, the more dangerous the journey.

When you return to the shop after a delivery, the warmth hits you again.
That contrast is intentional and central to the experience.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CORE LOOP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. Receive an order (order slip appears in the shop)
2. Cook the pizza (prep toppings, time the bake)
3. Step outside and deliver it (navigate to the address)
4. Return to the shop (collect payment, next order)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
COOKING PHASE (Inside the Shop)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Tone: Cozy, calm, lighthearted. No monsters can enter.

Mechanics (prototype):
- Place toppings on dough to fill the order
- Put pizza in oven, time the bake (too short = undercooked, too long = burned)
- Perfect cook = higher tip, better pizza quality

Pizza quality matters during delivery — a damaged or poor-quality pizza
means less money and unhappy customers.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
DELIVERY PHASE (Outside)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Tone: Dark, tense, dreadful. Silly but threatening (think Lethal Company / REPO).
      You are mostly vulnerable. The goal is to survive, not to fight.

Mechanics (prototype):
- Navigate from the shop to the delivery address on foot
- Monsters are attracted to you (and your pizza)
- Monsters can damage the pizza if they reach you (reduces payout)
- Reach the lit doorstep, deliver, return to shop
- No timer — the pressure comes from the monsters, not a clock

Monsters (prototype):
- Mindless drones, attracted to pizza smell
- They shamble toward you; too many = you're overwhelmed
- You can be knocked back or stunned; "death" costs you money/reputation, no hard game over

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PROGRESSION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Earn money from deliveries. Spend it on upgrades.

Transport ladder:
  On foot → Skateboard → Bicycle → Delivery Van

Each step changes the delivery experience: speed, range, ability to deal
with threats incidentally (e.g., the van can run over grunt mobs).

Basic defensive items (later):
- Simple knockback weapons (rolling pin, pizza cutter) — push mobs away, not kill
- Purpose is to create space and escape, not to fight

Combat is never the point. Upgrades make you less helpless, not powerful.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ATMOSPHERE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Music contrast is critical:
- Inside the shop: lofi, warm, nostalgic. Vaporwave / old Weather Channel vibes.
- Outside: ambient dread. Late-night tension. Think Phil Collins "In the Air Tonight"
  — that slow-burn feeling before something happens.

Visual contrast:
- Shop interior: warm oranges and yellows, visible and inviting
- Outside: dark, cool-toned, fog, distant lights

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FUTURE / OUT OF PROTOTYPE SCOPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Co-op multiplayer (asymmetric: cook vs. deliver, rescue missions)
- Neighborhood progression / map unlocking
- Topping variety with customer preferences
- Monster variety and factions
- Shop upgrades and fortification
- 3D version (this prototype is 2D)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PROTOTYPE GOAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Make the emotional loop feel right:
  Cozy kitchen → dread the moment you step outside → relief when you get back.

If that contrast lands, the game works. Everything else is built on top of it.
